Foldling Paper Pet: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Foldling Paper Pet is a small game about a paper pet, made and run by Rodrigo Motta Mendes, Estonia ("we"). This page says what the app does with your data. Contact: goodgrabsupport+foldling@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, your e-mail or your contacts.
  • Your game is saved on your phone only. We have no server and we never receive it.
  • The app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It works without the internet.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no in-app purchases.

What the app stores

The app saves your game: your Foldling, its garden, the visitors, the album with the photos you took of your visitors inside the game, the letters, and small notes such as which tips you have already seen. It also saves a few small pictures for the widget, so the widget can show how Foldling is doing. Nothing else.

The app reads your phone's clock (for the sky and bedtime) and, while it is open, the angle of the hinge and the tilt of the phone (for the pop-up page and the pinwheel). It uses them only inside the app and sends them nowhere. It never uses your camera, your microphone, your photos or your location.

Where it is stored

Only on your phone, inside the app's own storage (the widget's pictures in storage the app shares with its own widget). Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include it, like any app's data. Deleting the app deletes the game.

What is sent

Nothing. The app does not connect to the internet and sends no data to us or anyone else.

The Privacy policy and Help rows in the game open this website in your phone's web browser. The app itself sends nothing; the browser loads the page as it loads any other website.

No ads, no tracking, no accounts

The app shows no ads, does not track you, uses no analytics and needs no account or sign-in. There are no in-app purchases.

Children

Foldling is for all ages. It does not collect any personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing we can look up, correct or delete. Your game is on your phone: deleting the app deletes it. Questions: goodgrabsupport+foldling@gmail.com.

If you live in the European Union you can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

Changes

If this ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date before the new version of the app comes out.

Contact

Questions about this policy: goodgrabsupport+foldling@gmail.com. Help with the game: the Help page.
