Foldling Paper Pet: Help

Foldling Paper Pet is a little paper pet that lives in a pop-up paper garden on your phone. It follows your phone's real clock: it plays in the day, sleeps at night and is happy to see you when you come back.

Write to us

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game: goodgrabsupport+foldling@gmail.com. We read every message.

How to play

  • On iPhone Duo the hinge is the game: open the phone slowly and the garden rises piece by piece; lay it flat to see the whole page; fold it shut to close the book.
  • On an iPhone that does not fold, the page pops up by itself when you open the app (a tap opens it at once). Swipe up to lay the page flat, swipe down to fold it back up. Leaving the app is the same as folding the phone shut.
  • Tap Foldling to pat it. With the page flat, tap a + spot to choose a garden piece for your paper scraps.
  • A visitor may come for a piece you placed while the app is closed. Tap it to take its photo for your album.

The berry bubble and the catch round

When Foldling gets hungry, a berry shows in its thought bubble. Tap the bubble to start the catch round: drag the basket under the berries falling from the trees. Golden ones are worth more; leave the brown spotty ones. Foldling eats its catch, and each berry it eats gives you paper scraps for the garden.

Bedtime and sleep

  • From 20:30 Foldling gets ready for bed. Fold the phone shut (or leave the app) to tuck it into its little house.
  • Still awake at midnight, it falls asleep by itself. It sleeps until 7:00 in the morning, so at night there is little to do: come back in the morning, when a folded paper gift may be waiting.
  • In the day, when it is very tired, it takes a nap and wakes up by itself once it has rested.

The widget and StandBy

Add Foldling's widget to your Home Screen (touch and hold an empty spot, then add a widget) to see how Foldling is doing while you are away. In StandBy (your iPhone charging, locked and on its side) the widget keeps you company through the night.

My Foldling is gone

Your Foldling is saved on your phone only: we have no server, so we cannot bring it back from our side. Deleting the app deletes it. A backup of your phone (for example iCloud Backup) made while Foldling was there may include it, like any app's data, so restoring that backup may bring your Foldling back.

Privacy

The app has no accounts, shows no ads, sends nothing and keeps your game on your phone only. Read the Privacy Policy.
